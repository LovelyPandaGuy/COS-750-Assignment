## Notes (word for word)

1. “Draw or edit” is constrained to valid moves (place key in an empty slot, rotate at a node) so marking is reliable and fully keyboard operable — no freehand drawing.
2. EditLog is the screen-reader equivalent of the edited tree and lets feedback refer to a specific step (“step 2 rotated at 30 — the lowest unbalanced node is 20”).

## Keyboard and accessibility

- Tab to slot or node; Enter places key; R / L rotate selected; Ctrl+Z undo.
- EditLog is the text equivalent for screen readers.
- Placed node: dashed thick ring + text "placed by you".

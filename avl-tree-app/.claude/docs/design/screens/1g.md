## States shown

- Before replay: option 2 selected, replay held.
- After replay: PredictionReveal.

## Links

- Lock in / Skip → watch replay ([1f](1f.md)). Replay that step → the deciding step in [1f](1f.md).

## Notes (word for word)

1. Prompt appears before every replay (each new example and “replay”). Predictions are never scored or stored as marks.
2. Reveal compares prediction to what happened in neutral language and links to the relevant step.

## Keyboard and accessibility

- Options carry number keys 1–4 (`kbd` labels drawn); a node can also be picked in the tree.
- Prompt is modal-less; replay is disabled until answered or skipped.
- Selected option: thicker border + word "selected".

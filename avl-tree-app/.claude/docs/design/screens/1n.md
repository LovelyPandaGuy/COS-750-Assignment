## Notes (word for word)

1. Switch state always in words (ON/OFF), plus thumb position. Toggling replays the same insert sequence so the two shapes can be compared.
2. At 15 nodes Insert disables with “Tree is full (15). Clear or remove to keep exploring.” Stats are descriptive, not points.

## Keyboard and accessibility

- Switch state in words + thumb position. EventLog is aria-live.
- Inline messages for duplicates and out-of-range keys.
